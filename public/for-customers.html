NightRider
Hub Rider Info for Riders

For the people in the passenger seat

The idea: Courtesy, consistency, quality, and safety.

NightRider is a work-in-progress software demo. No transportation services are offered, and all trips are simulated. Only the code is live.

The core principle: Be what Rideshare was supposed to be: The Modern Taxi. Convenient, on demand, safe, and PROFESSIONAL above all else.

Our design goal: Ease of use, availability on any device, convenience, and data security via non-collection.

What matters to us

01 / COMFORT

Clean, comfortable, and calm.

A well-kept vehicle, a comfortable place to sit, and a quiet ride (unless you'd rather chat). Our bare minimum is what the other guys call Premium.

02 / DEPENDABILITY

Know what to expect.

Clear pickup information, realistic timing, and useful updates when plans change. We believe reliability starts before the car arrives.

03 / RESPECT & SAFETY

Professional on both sides of the door.

Smooth, safe, professional drivers only who meet the standard we believe transportation professionals should meet. Our drivers NEVER use voice calling for any reason while carrying passengers in motion. No beeping, blaring apps throwing rides at your driver while he's concentrating on getting you there safely.

04 / HONEST VALUE

Clarity beats gimmicks.

Trip details and fare estimates should make sense. Our algorithm is simple. 25¢ per mile to come to you, $1 per mile to take you there, and $5 on top per ride (flag drop or cancellation). No surging, no surprises.

A familiar driver beats another mystery ride.

We believe transportation works better when customers can recognize the people providing it and drivers have a reason to remember their customers. You shouldn't need to guess whether the person arriving takes their work seriously. All drivers are vetted, tested, and checked FAR above industry standards.

How to explore the demo:

No separate customer app to download. We don't even have a rider app, book a ride from any device. Click the RIDER tile and use username DEMO with password PASSWORD.

Use practice trip details.

Enter sample pickup and destination details and explore the booking workflow for now or later. Every request is a simulation; no transportation is arranged and no payment is collected.

Try the other sides.

Switch to the Driver and limited Dispatch test accounts to explore trip stages, maps, and conversations. Friends, family, potential riders and drivers, and anyone interested are welcome to try all the features with test data only.
