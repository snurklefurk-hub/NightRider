Company standards · Prelaunch plans

For insurers, investors, and clients

Our proposed onboarding standards, vehicle expectations, and road to opening a professional local transportation service. These are targets for a future operation, not claims of current insurance, licensing, approval, or active transportation service. Final eligibility will require applicable legal compliance, inspection, and insurer approval.

Driver requirements

In the past 7 years

  • No at-fault accidents resulting in a claim involving bodily injury to any person.
  • No at-fault accidents resulting in a claim for property damage exceeding $500.
  • No convictions for violations other than simple paperwork infractions or non-moving violations.
  • No speeding tickets for more than 10 mph above the posted speed limit.

In the past 3 years

Absolutely no tickets or convictions of any kind except minor paperwork infractions.

Experience and legal compliance

Drivers must have held a valid driver's license for more than 7 years at the time of onboarding.

Drivers and the company must also comply with applicable Georgia ride share and transportation network company requirements, including O.C.G.A. §§ 40-1-190 through 40-1-201 and applicable insurance provisions in O.C.G.A. § 33-1-24. These references are not an exhaustive legal checklist.

Vehicle requirements

We want room for cool, unusual, and classic cars that fall outside other platforms' strict model-year rules, while holding a higher overall standard for safety and comfort.

Vehicles under 15 years old in excellent mechanical and cosmetic condition are extremely likely to be eligible if they meet every requirement below, subject to inspection, underwriting, and legal approval:

  • Three adults can sit comfortably in the rear, each properly buckled, while the driver and front passenger also sit comfortably at a reasonable distance from their airbags.
  • Four side-entry doors.
  • Capacity for four passengers and four carry-on-sized bags simultaneously. Passenger seating may not be counted as luggage space unless those seats remain unoccupied while carrying four passengers.

One standard, one price structure

We do not intend to offer XL, XXL, Black, Comfort, or other upbadged tiers. We will not accept more than four passengers per ride or increase the fare based on vehicle features above the standard. Our sole standard tier is intended to closely match the comfort expectations commonly associated with Uber Comfort.

Classics, curiosities, and rare cars

We may permit exceptional older or unusual vehicles in the future. They must still meet the same four-passenger, luggage-capacity, and comfort standards, along with enhanced mechanical and cosmetic inspection, underwriting approval, and all insurer restrictions. No exception is guaranteed.

Roadmap

Today

The app and ecosystem are in development and are being tested against real-world conditions for stability and usability. No transportation services are currently offered.

Next landmark: Georgia compliance

Form the company, obtain the required state licenses and insurance to operate a transportation network company in Georgia, and prepare for the required regulatory review, including an in-person audit where applicable, before offering services.

After that: Service launch

Begin service in the Savannah area when authorized and operationally ready. Expand into Beaufort and Jasper counties only after meeting applicable South Carolina requirements.

These are proposed NightRider operating policies, not statements that Georgia mandates the same 7-year history, 3-year record, vehicle-age preference, or comfort thresholds. Final screening criteria, vehicle eligibility, insurance and audit procedures must be reviewed with the relevant regulators and underwriters before onboarding.
