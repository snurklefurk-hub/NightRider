Hub Rider Info for drivers

For the people behind the wheel

A professional ride should feel professional.

Good driving deserves more than a race to the lowest fare. We're building around professional standards and a driver's own reputation.

The future design goal is to help good drivers join a dependable, respectable business. A real job with the flex and lifestyle of a gig app, and less of the uncertainty and headache.

The design starts with professionalism, independence, and transparent expectations.

The standard comes first.

01 / PRIDE

Take pride in your work.

A clean, comfortable, quiet vehicle. A courteous driver. Thoughtful driving. Showing up prepared and treating every ride like your reputation depends on it, because it does.

02 / RELIABILITY

We do what we say and say what we mean.

Clear pickup details, dependable communication, realistic expectations, and service the Drivers and Riders can count on. A professional ride doesn't need unnecessary surprises.

03 / RESPECT

Standards go both ways.

Drivers deserve safe working conditions and respectful customers. Riders deserve a safe vehicle, a properly qualified driver, and respectful service.

04 / TRANSPARENCY

No race to the bottom.

We don't believe winning means being the cheapest option in town. You'll never bid or fight other drivers over who can do it cheaper and faster. One standard of service, no rush, no shoving the next ride in your face as you're trying to navigate an unfamiliar subdivision, no tricks, no carrot, no stick. Actual fares will be determined after we understand commercial insurance, licensing, and operating costs.

Independence and the bigger picture.

05 / RELATIONSHIPS

Build something worth keeping.

Uber and Lyft can still fill empty hours. But when a customer books you directly, that relationship should be worth nurturing. A returning passenger who trusts you is more valuable than an endless stream of anonymous, one-off trips and platforms itching to fire you to make room for the next guy willing to do it for less.

06 / THE BOTTOM LINE

A modern taxi, not another Uber.

Bottom line: We don't want to be the next Uber. We want to be what rideshare should have been: the modern evolution of the classic taxi; Non professionals with non livery cars being able to step into the industry without overburdening regulations, empowered by technology.
