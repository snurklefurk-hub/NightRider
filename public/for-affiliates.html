Affiliates and Partners

We're looking forward to working with other companies in the future.

Hotels, resorts, bars, clubs, restaurants, anybody that wants us to provide our services for their clientele.

The booking portal, potential partner arrangements, and client handoff features below are planned, not live tools. No passenger fares or partner compensation terms have been set.

A third-party portal built for your clientele

We plan to offer a robust third-party portal where you can request a ride, see upfront pricing and availability, and either check out on your device or push everything to your client with an SMS message.

That link would take them to NightRider with the trip ready to review and a one-click account setup or sign-in experience (assuming they don't already have an account), so they can check out on their own device. Any account creation and messaging would require appropriate customer consent.

Potential partner arrangements

We're exploring whether referral or concierge arrangements would make sense for local partners. No fees, commissions, or passenger fares are established. Any future terms will depend on commercial insurance, regulatory compliance, operating costs, and clear fare disclosures to riders.

All third-party rides will integrate seamlessly into the NightRider ecosystem, and our guys will handle it from there.

A real local company. A real dispatcher.

You're not just sending a ride request out into the aether and hoping one of the big platforms finds somebody to take it. Our vision is for you to deal directly with a real local transportation company, with real people who know the area and take responsibility for your client's ride.

When NightRider begins offering transportation, the plan is to have a live dispatcher always on duty while we're accepting bookings, someone you can reach to answer questions, coordinate a pickup, and follow through when plans change. Your request won't just disappear into an algorithm.

Accessibility remains part of the standard

While we have no plans to offer non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT), we intend to meet applicable ADA accessibility and nondiscrimination requirements for regular rides.
